About us

From 30 litres a day to a dairy thousands of families rely on

Shambani Milk Limited grew out of a simple belief: Tanzanian families deserve milk they can trust.

Our story

Shambani began in 2003 as Shambani Graduates Enterprises — a small milk processing effort started by founder Victor Mfinanga with just two employees and 30 litres of milk a day.

Word spread. Families across Morogoro came to rely on Shambani's honest, unadulterated milk, and in 2015 the business incorporated as Shambani Milk Limited, opening a new chapter of growth with equity investment from MTI Investment of Stockholm.

Today our factory in Tungi, Morogoro processes up to 8,000 litres a day and serves Morogoro, Dar es Salaam, Dodoma and Pwani — still guided by the same founding promise: real milk, honestly made.

Our mission

To make quality dairy products accessible to people across Tanzania while directly supporting local smallholder producers — every litre we buy keeps a farming family growing.

Our quality promise

No adulteration, no shortcuts. Every pack of Shambani milk, mtindi and yoghurt is pasteurised, homogenised and quality-checked, so what reaches your family is exactly what nature made.

Real dairy from Morogoro — fresh milk, mtindi and yoghurt from ~400 family farms to your table.
